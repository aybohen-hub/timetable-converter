01 · Export

Open the Optima app or the Timetablers website and sign in to your account. Open your timetable, click the three dots in the top-right corner, then choose “Download My Timetable” → “Grid” → “HTML”. Include every week you want to import.

02 · Convert

Choose the file, review your classes and set your reminder.

03 · Import

Open the .ics in Apple Calendar, or use “Import” in Google Calendar or Outlook. Use a separate calendar for your timetable.

Privacy & updating your timetable

No uploads, analytics, cookies or accounts. Files are read in this browser only, and cleared when you close or reload the page. The hosting provider may log ordinary page visits.

This is a snapshot, not a live subscription. Stable event IDs do not guarantee that calendar apps replace previous imports. For an update, remove the old timetable events from your separate calendar, then import the new file. Reminder support varies by calendar app.

Room labels such as “Lab” and “Seminar Room” describe the room, not necessarily the teaching format. This independent student tool is not affiliated with Lancaster University.
